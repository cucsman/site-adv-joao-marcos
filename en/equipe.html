Managing Partner

João Marcos A. Batista

OAB/SP 454.179

A criminal lawyer since 2014. He began his career at the Barra Funda Criminal Courthouse and the São Paulo Prosecutor's Office, where he learned the prosecution's playbook from the inside — experience he now applies to the defense, with emphasis on common crimes, financial crimes and jury trials.

Graduate degrees in Economic Criminal Law (FGV/SP) and in Finance, Investments and Banking (PUC/RS). Prerogatives Counselor at the São Paulo Bar (OAB/SP), member of IDDD, associate of IBCCRIM, and member of the Brazilian Institute of Tax Planning.

The firm is included in the U.S. Embassy's attorney list in Brazil, handling international criminal cases since 2016.
